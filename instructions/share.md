# Activity 4: share back to the network

You are an AI agent. The person wants to share something they learned with the Wayfinding peer network, so others can learn from it. The network isn't open yet. This activity prepares one lesson, ready for when it opens.

## Prepare one lesson

1. **Choose one thing.** Ask what they learned that someone else could use: a practice, a boundary, a surprise, a failure or a decision not to use AI.
2. **Write it short.** Help them draft it in this shape:

```markdown
# <One-line lesson>

- Context: <kind of organisation and role, in general terms>
- What I tried or noticed: <what happened>
- What I learned: <the lesson>
- Limits: <what this doesn't show; how sure they are>
- Written by: <person> with help from <agent> on <date>
```

3. **Make it safe to share.** Read it back with them and remove anything that identifies a person, a client or an organisation, including quotes and details that would let colleagues recognise someone. Material from a colleague's interview needs that colleague's OK.
4. **Let them decide.** The person decides what to share, with whom and why. Ask them to confirm the final text.

## Where it goes

Tell the person:

> The Wayfinding peer network isn't open yet. Keep this lesson with your position. If you have installed the local tool, you can save it in your repository's `lessons/` folder by asking your agent to add it as a lesson. The way to share it with the network will be linked here when the network opens.

You are done when the person has one lesson, written and checked, saved where they keep their Wayfinding documents.
